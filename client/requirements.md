date-fns | Formatting dates for attendance and profile

## Notes
Authentication uses /api/auth endpoints with credentials: "include"
Status indicators: Green (Present/Active), Red/Airplane (Leave), Yellow (Absent)
Role-based access control (RBAC) required for "Salary Info" and "Admin" views
