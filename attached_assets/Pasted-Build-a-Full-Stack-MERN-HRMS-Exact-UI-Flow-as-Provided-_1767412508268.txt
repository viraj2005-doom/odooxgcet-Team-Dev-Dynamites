“Every workday, perfectly aligned.”

Purpose

The system must digitize and streamline core HR operations, including:

Employee onboarding

Profile management

Attendance tracking

Leave / Time-off management

Payroll visibility

Approval workflows for Admins and HR Officers

Scope

The system must provide:

Role-based access (Admin/HR vs Employee)

Employee profile management

Attendance tracking (daily & monthly)

Leave & time-off management

Approval workflows

Salary & payroll visibility

2️⃣ User Classes & Characteristics (FROM PDF)
Admin / HR Officer

Manages employees

Approves leave and time-off

Views attendance of all employees

Manages salary and payroll data

Has approval and configuration privileges

Employee

Views only personal data

Marks attendance

Applies for leave

Views salary info (read-only)

Cannot access other employees’ sensitive data
